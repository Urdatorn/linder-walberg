Hand transcriptions that replace a strip of the OCR: `NNNN_SS.txt` replaces
strip SS of page NNNN (an empty file drops the strip). Paragraphs are
separated by blank lines.

- `0539_4`–`0539_9`: the Tryckfel table at the foot of printed page 535, read
  from the scan (the model looped on it). Rows are tab-separated. Its
  corrections are in `corrections/errata.tsv`, anchored to their entries.
